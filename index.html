SpendLocker

A personal finance & document vault desktop app.

SpendLocker runs entirely on your own computer and helps you track expenses, investments, budgets, and financial documents (receipts, invoices, statements) in a single AES-256 encrypted local vault. There is no server — your data never leaves your device unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.

What it does

  • Tracks expenses and investments with dashboards, budgets, and category breakdowns
  • Stores documents (receipts, invoices, statements) in an encrypted local vault
  • Optionally backs up documents to your own Google Drive
  • Optionally imports email attachments (receipts/invoices) from your own Gmail inbox via IMAP

Availability

SpendLocker is a Java desktop application, distributed directly to its users as a native installer (macOS .dmg, Windows .exe). It is not a web app and has no public sign-up — there is nothing to log into on this page.
